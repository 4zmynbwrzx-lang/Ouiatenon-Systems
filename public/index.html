01 — Current work

Civil3D-AI

A Civil 3D add-in that lets an engineer say what they need in plain language and have it carried out in the drawing they already have open.

The goal is to take repetitive drafting and setup work off the engineer's plate, so more of the day goes to the engineering itself.

02 — Approach

Built from the drafting table out

  1. A

    Start from real workflows

    Tools are shaped by time spent in a civil engineering office, not by guesses about what engineers do.

  2. B

    Engineers stay in charge

    Software should do the tedious parts and leave judgment, review and sign-off with the people accountable for the design.

  3. C

    Fit the tools you already use

    Work happens inside Civil 3D and existing drawings, so there's nothing new to migrate to.
